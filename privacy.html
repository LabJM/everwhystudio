Privacy Policy

Everwhy Studio · Last updated October 10, 2026

Everwhy Studio makes videos for its own channels (Why Design Works, Evergreen Living, Capital Campus and Everwhy Toons). We use a private publishing tool, run on our own computer, to upload our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X. On Google's sign-in screen the tool is shown as "Youtube Auto Uploader". This policy explains what that tool does with data.

Who uses the tool

Only Everwhy Studio. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data we access

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic account information (such as the account ID and display name) for that account.

The tool uses them to publish our own videos with their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, to post our own comment under our own videos, to check whether a post finished, and to read the view, like and comment counts of our own posts so we can see how they perform.

On YouTube, the tool also reads public information about public videos (search results, titles, view counts and top public comments) to research topics for our channels. This public information is used only for our own planning. It is never used to contact, track or build a profile of anyone.

We do not collect or store personal information about viewers or other users, and we do not access messages or followers' personal data.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and the video files stay on our own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. We delete a token when we disconnect the account, and you can remove the tool's access at any time from the platform's own settings.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through the tool follows the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the tool's access to a Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Platforms

Content we publish is also subject to the privacy policies of YouTube, Instagram (Meta), TikTok and X.
