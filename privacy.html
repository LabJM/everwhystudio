Privacy Policy

Everwhy Studio · Last updated October 7, 2026

Everwhy Studio makes educational short videos for its own channels (Why Design Works, Evergreen Living and Capital Campus). We use a private publishing tool, run on our own computer, to upload our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X. This policy explains what that tool does with data.

Who uses the tool

Only Everwhy Studio. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What data we access

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic account information (such as the account ID and display name) for that account. The tool uses them only to publish our own videos and captions and to check whether a post finished.

We do not collect, read or store information about viewers or any other users, and we do not access messages, followers' data, or analytics through the tool.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and the video files stay on our own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. We delete a token when we disconnect the account, and you can remove the tool's access at any time from the platform's own settings.

Platforms

Content we publish is also subject to the privacy policies of YouTube, Instagram (Meta), TikTok and X.

Contact
