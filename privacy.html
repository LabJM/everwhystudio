Privacy Policy

Everwhy Studio · Last updated October 10, 2026

Everwhy Studio makes videos for its own channels. Everwhy Studio Uploader is our private publishing tool. It runs only on our own computer and posts our own videos to our own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X. This policy explains what the tool does with data.

Who uses the tool

Only Everwhy Studio. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

Data from TikTok

Login Kit (user.info.basic): we connect our own TikTok account with Login Kit for Desktop. TikTok gives the tool an access token, and the tool reads the account's open ID, display name and avatar only to show which account is connected.

Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish): the tool uploads our own videos and publishes them to our own TikTok account. Before each post it reads the creator info TikTok provides (nickname, available privacy levels, comment, Duet and Stitch settings, and maximum video length) to show TikTok's posting settings. Nothing is posted until we press "Post to TikTok".

The tool does not read other TikTok users' data, messages, followers or comments.

Data from other platforms

On YouTube, Instagram and X the tool publishes our own videos with their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, posts our own comment under our own videos, and reads the view, like and comment counts of our own posts. On YouTube it also reads public information about public videos (search results, view counts and top public comments) to research topics, used only for our own planning.

We do not collect or store personal information about viewers or other users.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and the video files stay on our own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. We delete a token when we disconnect the account, and the account owner can remove the tool's access at any time from the platform's own settings.

Platforms

Content we publish is also subject to the privacy policies of TikTok, YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and X.
