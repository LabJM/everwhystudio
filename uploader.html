Everwhy Studio Uploader

Our in-house publishing tool. It is not offered to the public.

Everwhy Studio Uploader is a private tool that runs on our own computer. When a video is finished, it publishes that video to Everwhy Studio's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X at the same time, with the title, description and cover we prepared. Nobody else signs in to it, and there is no sign-up.

What it does on TikTok

  • Login Kit for Desktop (user.info.basic): connects our own TikTok account and shows which account is connected.
  • Content Posting API: uploads and publishes our own videos to our own TikTok account.

Before each TikTok post, the tool shows TikTok's posting settings: the connected account, a preview of the video, an editable title, the privacy level (we choose it each time, there is no default), and the comment, Duet and Stitch settings the account allows. Sponsored videos are disclosed as branded content, AI-generated videos carry TikTok's AI-generated label, and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation is shown. Nothing is posted until we press "Post to TikTok".

Other platforms

On YouTube, Instagram and X the tool publishes our own videos, posts our own comment under them, and reads the view, like and comment counts of our own posts.

Data

Access tokens and video files stay on our own computer. The tool does not collect data about other users and nothing is sold or shared. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
