How we make a video

The same four steps for every video.

Research

Every claim is checked against primary or trusted sources: patents, standards, official reports, museum archives and peer-reviewed studies.

Script

One question per video, a few verified facts, and the answer explained step by step in plain English.

Visuals

3D scenes and motion graphics made with AI video tools and our own code, labeled as AI-generated wherever the platform offers a label.

Publish

Each video goes to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X at the same time. The YouTube description lists its sources.
