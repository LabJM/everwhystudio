About Everwhy Studio

An independent studio for short educational videos.

Everwhy Studio is a small independent studio that makes educational videos for its own channels. Our videos take one question about something familiar, such as why plane windows are round or why IKEA stores feel like a maze, and answer it with verified facts and clear visuals.

Our standards

  • Facts first. We only state what our sources support, and we list the sources in each video's YouTube description.
  • Plain language. We explain the idea itself, in words anyone can follow, and name the rule or principle behind it.
  • AI is labeled. Our visuals are made with AI video tools and our own code, and our posts carry the AI-generated content label wherever the platform offers one.
  • No advice. We do not give medical, legal or investment advice.
  • Clear sponsorship. Any sponsored video is disclosed as paid partnership or branded content on every platform.

How we make a video

The same four steps for every video.

Research

Every claim is checked against primary or trusted sources: patents, standards, official reports, museum archives and peer-reviewed studies.

Script

One question per video, a few verified facts, and the answer explained step by step in plain English.

Visuals

3D scenes and motion graphics made with AI video tools and our own code, labeled as AI-generated wherever the platform offers a label.

Publish

Each video goes to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X at the same time. The YouTube description lists its sources.
