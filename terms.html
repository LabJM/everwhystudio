Everwhy Studio · Last updated October 10, 2026

These terms cover Everwhy Studio Uploader, the publishing tool Everwhy Studio uses to post its own videos.

The tool

The tool runs on Everwhy Studio's own computer. It uploads videos that Everwhy Studio made to accounts that Everwhy Studio owns on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X, together with their titles and captions. It is for our internal use only and is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

Posting to TikTok

Each TikTok post is made from TikTok's posting settings shown in the tool: the connected account's nickname, a preview of the video, an editable title, a privacy level we choose ourselves (there is no default), and the comment, Duet and Stitch settings the account allows. Commercial content is disclosed: sponsored videos are marked as Branded content. Videos made with AI tools carry TikTok's AI-generated label. By posting, we agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, for Branded content, TikTok's Branded Content Policy. Nothing is posted until we press "Post to TikTok".

Platforms

Use of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X through the tool also follows each platform's own terms of service and developer terms, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Everwhy Studio does not use the tool to post on behalf of other people, to send spam, or to collect other users' data.
